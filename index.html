About

I'm Tom Bailey, a freelance 3D artist working in live events.

I build 3D assets, environments and animation for tours and festivals: content for the big screens, digital replicas of artwork and sets, and concept renders of stage pieces before they're built. I also model custom pieces for 3D printing, like stage props.

Photography is where I study light, composition and form, and it shapes everything I make in 3D. See my photography →
